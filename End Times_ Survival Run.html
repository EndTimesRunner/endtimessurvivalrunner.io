    Outrun the Darkness

    The darkness pursues you with every step. Beat your target time or face the consequences. Every run is a race against an inevitable force that grows stronger as you slow down.

    Collect Light Fragments

    Gather mystical Light Fragments at checkpoints during your runs. Use them to unlock exclusive death marker packs, special emojis, and other rewards in the shop.

    The Graveyard

    Explore an interactive memorial to fallen runners. Place your own unique grave marker if you fall, complete with custom epitaphs and death emojis. Attempt Revival Runs to save others.

    Level Up & Progress

    Earn XP with every run, level up your character, and unlock achievements. Track your personal bests, maintain survival streaks, and compete on global leaderboards.

    Custom Goals

    Create custom running segments with personalized distances and target times. Set your own challenges and watch as the darkness adapts to your performance.

    Health Integration

    Connect Google Fit or Apple Health to track your daily vitals, steps, and heart points. Unlock the "Let's Stay Fit" achievement and monitor your overall wellness journey.

    Privacy & Data Usage

    Your privacy is sacred to us. Here's exactly how we use your data:

    Google Fit Integration (Optional)

    If you choose to connect Google Fit, we access your daily step count, heart points, calories burned, and distance walked/run to display in your Vitals dashboard. This helps you track your overall fitness progress alongside your survival runs.

    GPS Tracking

    Location data is only collected during active runs with your explicit permission. This data is used to track your route, calculate distance, and place grave markers if you fall.

    No Selling, No Ads, No Tracking

    We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never track you across other apps or websites. Your data stays yours.
